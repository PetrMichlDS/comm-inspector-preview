What it checks

Every call classified, every regulated step verified

AI Call Inspector categorises each call and confirms that regulated conversations followed the rules — across 100% of your volume, not a sample.

  • Multi-level call categorisation by topic, product, and outcome
  • Identification of clients, products, and regulated processes
  • Verification of regulatory requirements, e.g. KYC, MiFID II.
  • GDPR and consent handling verified in the conversation
  • Promotion conditions communicated correctly and in full
  • Low-confidence cases flagged for a human, never force-decided
