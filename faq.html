AI Comms Inspector analyses customer conversations and turns them into structured, actionable insights for CRM, contact centre, customer experience, sales, compliance and risk processes.

It can process call recordings, meeting recordings, existing transcripts, chats, emails and agent notes. Audio can be converted into text before analysis.

It can detect customer needs, life events, sales opportunities, product feedback, service issues, compliance risks and process gaps based on business questions defined with your team.

The solution captures communication data, prepares and analyses it against predefined questions, and delivers the results as structured data, dashboards, alerts or signals for other business systems.
