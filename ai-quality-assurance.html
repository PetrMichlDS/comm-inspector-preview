AI Quality Assurance

Supervises whether agents follow your sales or support process, scores conversation quality across calls and chat, and monitors performance across teams and agents.

Book a walkthrough
What it supervises

Your playbook, applied to every conversation

AI Quality Assurance scores each conversation against your own sales or support process — across 100% of interactions, not a random sample.

  • Agents follow the defined sales or support process
  • Chat-support quality supervised alongside calls
  • Sales-conversation quality scored across calls and chat
  • Performance monitored across teams and individual agents
  • Consistent scoring criteria applied to every interaction
  • Low-confidence cases flagged for human review, never force-scored
How teams use it

From spot-checks to full oversight

Team leads

See where the process breaks down and which conversations need attention — without listening to calls at random.

Comparable scores

The same quality criteria applied to 100% of interactions, so scores are consistent across teams and over time.

Human in the loop

Uncertain cases are surfaced for a reviewer to decide, rather than being scored on a guess.
